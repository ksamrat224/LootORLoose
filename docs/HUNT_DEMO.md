# Hunt demo script and review checklist

Target a roughly two-minute gameplay walkthrough plus a 20-second future-scope close; this is a script, not a recorded demo. Use fictional demo credits only. Do not promise fixed reveal times, zero wallet popups, guaranteed returns, or that cosmetic choices change rewards.

1. **Entrance:** “One stake, three discoveries.” Show the demo-credit label. Practice is immediately playable; Devnet uses MagicBlock. If required Phantom checks are still pending, use the isolated release candidate for V6 and say so.
2. **Prepare:** Choose $2 and Start hunt. In Devnet, show the actual preparation steps and count real wallet approvals. Explain that the short-lived session authorizes quest opening only; collection still needs the wallet.
3. **Choose a route:** Open Journal. Show six guardian slots and an optional route goal. Pick North Trail (Copper Watch → Obsidian Keep → Crown Vault) for a compact demonstration. Explain that every quest shares the same prize pool and badges do not change returns.
4. **Explore:** Walk toward a numbered guardian. Point out that floor detail brightens while every path and marker stays visible. Choose Open quest explicitly. Accept the actual generated reward, including zero. Show its lore entry unlocking.
5. **Reveal:** Open two more distinct guardians. In Devnet, explain that the delegated hunt and session signing avoid asking the wallet for each authorized reveal. Describe only the timing observed during this run; do not treat prior measurements as a guarantee.
6. **Verify and collect:** Show Proof of roll and version-specific Reward rules. Collect once, await confirmed credit, then show total return, net change, confirmed demo balance and receipt. Show the route badge after collection. Returning to the dungeon never starts another hunt.
7. **Future scope (after the completed result):** Show a simple title card headed **Future concept — not in this build** with “Distinct guardian rooms · hazards and health · inventory choices · timed extraction · verified leaderboard.” Say: “The current hunt proves the core loop: explore, reveal verifiable loot, and collect once. Our next gameplay direction gives guardians different challenges, adds hazards and inventory choices, and asks players to reach an extraction portal before time runs out. A later competitive mode could rank verified runs. Those mechanics need a separate rules version and fairness review; they are concepts, not features of this build.” The future design is described in [dungeon-upgrade-phases.md](../dungeon-upgrade-phases.md).

If Devnet is delayed, show recovery or switch to practice. Explain that the existing pending request is retained; do not pretend the practice result came from VRF. Controlled client interruptions are not provider-outage evidence.
